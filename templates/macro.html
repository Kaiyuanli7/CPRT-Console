Total-loss pressure

Download chart

A car is totaled when repairing it costs too much relative to what it's worth. This index divides repair prices by used-car prices (2019 = 100). Above 100, repairs have become relatively more expensive, so more damaged cars become salvage: the structural tailwind in the long thesis.
