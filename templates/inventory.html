If the automatic scan is blocked

Copart sometimes shows automated browsers a bot check. The console then stops for the day instead of trying to get around it. To fill the gap, scan with it: a Copart window opens on all lots. In the filters on the left, open Newly added lots and choose Last 7 days. When the results update, close the window. The console saves the same counts the automatic scan would.

What the numbers are: counts Copart shows next to its search results, covering every kind of seller (insurers, dealers, rental fleets, charities), because Copart hides sellers from logged-out visitors. "Added in the last 7 days" is Copart's own filter: lots assigned to a sale in the last 7 days that are still listed, so lots that sold within the week aren't in it. Compare it week to week rather than reading it as total arrivals. Yards outside the US are left out of the state figures. Every scan's raw response is kept in the data folder.
